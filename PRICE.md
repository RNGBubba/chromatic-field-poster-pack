# Chromatic Field — print-ready pack

## Suggested offer

**US$18** for the digital print pack: four original generative poster systems, source code, deterministic seeds, and a personal-use print license. A commercial license can be offered separately for studios that want to sell physical prints.

## Print specification

- Four compositions, 3:4 aspect ratio
- Target physical size: 18 × 24 inches
- Print-ready target: **300 DPI** (5400 × 7200 px)
- Color: RGB source; convert to the printer's requested profile before production
- Export: lossless PNG from the browser, then upscale/render at the target dimensions
- No raster art assets, fonts, or proprietary resources are bundled
